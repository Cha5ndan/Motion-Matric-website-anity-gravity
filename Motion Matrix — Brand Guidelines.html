The marks

Logo & lockups.

The mark is a square within a square — a matrix framing the work. Two approved colorways: black on light, white on dark. The wordmark is the primary lockup; the glyph stands alone only at small sizes or as an avatar.

Clear space
& misuse.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the glyph's inner square on all sides. Minimum: glyph 24 px, wordmark 96 px wide.

✕ Never
  • Recolor outside the two approved colorways
  • Rotate, stretch, or condense the mark
  • Add drop shadows or outlines to the logo itself
  • Place on busy imagery without a solid backing
  • Pair the seal directly beside the wordmark
✓ Always
  • Use full-resolution PNG/SVG from assets/
  • Maintain clear space and minimum sizes
  • Reserve the seal for stamps & print accents

Typography

Two families.
Max contrast.

Display · Anton
Aa

Tall, condensed, all-caps. Headlines and section starters. Tracked tight (−2%). Treat it like architecture.

Body · Inter
Aa Bb Cc

Humanist, neutral. 400 for body, 500 for UI, 600–700 for sub-heads. Gets out of the way.

Scale

96 / AntonHeadline 64 / AntonSection 28 / Inter 700Heading three 16 / Inter 400Body paragraph copy 12 / Archivo 600Eyebrow tag JetBrains+218% · 14d

The "contrast" is between the families — huge condensed display next to small humanist body. Never two display faces, never two body faces.

Voice & tone

Confident.
Plain. Partner-first.

We sound like a smart freelance friend who happens to run a studio — not a corporate agency. "Your" is the most important word in the brand. The customer is the protagonist; we're the operator behind them. Short sentences. One idea each. We, not I.

"We build brands that ship."
"Tell us where it hurts."
"Two weeks. One brand. Done."

Visual language

The motifs.

Hard offset shadow
The signature. 6px 6px 0 #000, no blur. A print-style drop shadow on featured cards, frames, and stickered CTAs. Neon variant on hover.
Three-band header
From the letterhead — black → coal → steel, left to right. A recurring device atop dark layouts and covers.
Buttons · sharp corners
Neon fill, 2px black border, hard shadow. Hover shifts up-left; press sticks down-right. The glyph is a square — radii stay at 0–4 px.
Highlight
Neon as marker
Use neon to highlight one word or underline a phrase — like a marker stripe. Flat fills only; never gradient the green.
